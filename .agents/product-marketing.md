# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-08-30

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** ASTRA is an AI system administrator that turns live Windows endpoint evidence into safe, verified IT action.
**What it does:** ASTRA collects fleet telemetry, searches enterprise knowledge, diagnoses endpoint problems, applies only permitted remediations, and verifies the result. Technomate also provides managed IT services and business hardware, giving customers one accountable implementation partner.
**Product category:** AI-powered endpoint management, IT operations automation, and managed IT services.
**Product type:** B2B SaaS plus optional managed services and hardware procurement.
**Business model:** Per-device SaaS at $4.49–$8.99 monthly or $44.90–$89.90 annually, with a full-product 14-day trial and no credit card; services and hardware are quoted separately.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Growing organizations with roughly 25–500 Windows endpoints, lean IT teams, distributed employees, recurring support demand, and a need for auditable automation. Initial geographic strength is India, especially NCR, with SaaS available more broadly.
**Decision-makers:** IT managers, heads of IT, operations leaders, founders/COOs at smaller firms, MSP owners, and security/compliance influencers.
**Primary use case:** Reduce repetitive endpoint tickets and resolution time without allowing unsupervised AI to perform sensitive actions.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Detect and resolve routine Windows issues before they disrupt employees.
- Give a small IT team one live view of endpoint health, actions, and approvals.
- Automate patching, inventory, offboarding, and remediation with an audit trail.
**Use cases:** Endpoint monitoring, self-healing, patch management, helpdesk escalation, secure offboarding, asset inventory, and fleet reporting.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Endpoint issues are frequent, evidence collection is slow, and routine support consumes skilled technician time.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Traditional RMM tools surface alerts but still require technicians to investigate and act.
- Generic AI assistants may explain a fix but lack live device evidence and enforceable action controls.
- Break/fix support reacts after disruption and creates unpredictable response times.
**What it costs them:** Lost employee time, technician context switching, prolonged incidents, and inconsistent remediation.
**Emotional tension:** IT leaders want automation but fear losing control, creating security exposure, or buying another dashboard nobody has time to monitor.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Atera, NinjaOne and other AI-enabled endpoint platforms; they have established distribution and broad integrations, while ASTRA differentiates on evidence-first reasoning and explicit remediation governance.
**Secondary:** Microsoft Intune, ManageEngine Endpoint Central, Freshservice and conventional RMM/ITSM stacks; they solve parts of the workflow but often require more manual coordination.
**Indirect:** Internal scripts, additional helpdesk hiring, local break/fix providers, and doing nothing.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Evidence-before-action workflow using knowledge plus live telemetry.
- Automatic, approval-required, and admin-only tiers enforced in backend code.
- Allowlisted device commands, mutation auditing, and post-action verification.
- SaaS plus India-based managed services and hardware delivery from one company.
**How we do it differently:** ASTRA treats remediation as a governed decision loop, not a chatbot suggestion or unbounded script runner.
**Why that's better:** Teams gain automation while retaining human control over risk.
**Why customers choose us:** A focused Windows workflow, transparent pricing, low-friction trial, and optional hands-on implementation.
