# ASTRA India-Wide Outbound Campaign

Updated: 2026-08-30

## Campaign objective

Generate qualified replies from Indian organizations with 25-500 Windows endpoints and convert suitable accounts into a free, guided 14-day ASTRA pilot for up to 10 devices.

The first-touch CTA is a reply, not an immediate meeting request. A booking link is shared after interest is confirmed.

## Ideal customer profile

Target an India-based organization with a meaningful Windows fleet, a lean internal IT or operations team, recurring endpoint support work, and a reason to improve visibility or reduce manual triage now.

### Required fit

- India-based or operating a material team in India
- Approximately 25-500 Windows endpoints; prioritize 50-500
- Business devices managed by an IT, operations, infrastructure or security owner
- Buyer role identifiable: Head of IT, IT Manager, Infrastructure Manager, COO or founder in a smaller company
- Public business contact source and collection date recorded

### Priority buying signals

1. Hiring IT support, system administration, infrastructure or endpoint roles
2. Opening a new office, expanding headcount or enabling distributed work
3. Public evidence of Microsoft 365, Intune, ManageEngine, RMM or helpdesk usage
4. Recent acquisition, migration, compliance initiative or device refresh
5. A visible lean IT team supporting a growing employee base

### Disqualifiers

- Consumer support, personal-device repair or primarily non-Windows fleet
- No identifiable business need or no recent buying signal
- Business-critical environment requiring certifications or integrations ASTRA does not currently support
- Contact obtained from a private/personal address, breached data or an unprovenanced list
- Contact email not verified before send

- **Cold (<60):** hold; do not send
- A prospect cannot be Hot without a recent signal and verified contact.

## Research and compliance workflow

1. Discover candidates using company sites, job pages, industry directories, news and licensed B2B tools.
2. Cross-check company size, India operations and the buying signal with at least two sources where possible.
3. Retain only public business-context details needed for outreach.
4. Record source URL, collection date and legitimate-interest rationale.
5. Verify the email address with a deliverability service before sending.
6. Exclude personal email addresses, risky/catch-all addresses that cannot be validated, and all opt-outs.
7. Send manually in small batches during the prospect's local business hours.
8. Honor an opt-out immediately and suppress the contact from future campaigns.
9. Delete non-responsive prospect data after 90 days unless a documented business reason requires shorter retention.

This is operational guidance, not legal advice. Obtain legal review before high-volume outreach or outreach outside India.

| Day | Activity | Volume | Measurement |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Monday | Research and qualify accounts | 10 candidates | Hot/Warm count |

| Wednesday | Publish LinkedIn education post | 1 | Impressions, comments, pilot clicks |
| Thursday | Send scheduled follow-ups | 5-10 | Replies, opt-outs |
| Friday | Engage with target-account posts manually | 5 accounts | Relevant conversations |

Start with no more than 10 new contacts per business day from a properly configured business mailbox. Pause if bounce rate exceeds 2%, spam complaints occur, or opt-outs rise unexpectedly.

## Funnel and decision rules

Track:

- researched accounts
- verified Hot accounts
- emails delivered
- positive replies
- pilot scopes requested
- demos booked
- qualified opportunities
- pilot starts

After the first 30 verified contacts:

- **Low delivery:** stop and fix list/email verification.
- **Delivery but no replies:** review signal quality and personalization before rewriting the offer.
- **Replies but no pilot interest:** inspect qualification and objections.
- **Pilot interest but no starts:** reduce onboarding friction and clarify ownership.
