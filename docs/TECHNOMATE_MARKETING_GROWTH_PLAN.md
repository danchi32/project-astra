# Technomate Marketing Growth Plan

Updated: 2026-08-30

## Objective

Generate qualified demos and ASTRA sign-ups from India-based SMBs and lean IT teams managing Windows endpoints. The primary conversion is a booked 30-minute demo; product sign-up and the offboarding checklist are secondary conversions.

## Positioning

**Category:** AI-powered endpoint operations and managed IT.

**Ideal customer:** Organizations with 25–500 Windows devices, especially teams with one to five IT staff, recurring endpoint tickets, distributed employees, and no appetite for a heavyweight enterprise rollout.

**Core promise:** ASTRA turns endpoint evidence into safe, verified action so IT teams prevent routine tickets while retaining human control over sensitive fixes.

**Differentiation:** Evidence-before-action reasoning, code-enforced approval tiers, allowlisted remediation, verification after every fix, plus optional India-based managed IT and hardware support.

Do not lead with all three business lines at equal weight. Lead with ASTRA's outcome and use managed services and hardware as implementation advantages.

## Market findings

- The competitive set is broader than AI assistants: NinjaOne, Atera, ManageEngine Endpoint Central, Microsoft Intune, Freshservice and regional managed-service providers all compete for the same budget.
- Mature competitors emphasize automated endpoint management, transparency, fast time-to-value, and reduced technician workload. Feature parity alone will not win.
- Regional providers compete heavily on low monthly pricing. Technomate should avoid a lowest-price position and instead sell controlled automation, measurable response time, and one accountable partner.
- Buyers need proof before they trust autonomous remediation. The first proof assets should be a real pilot result, security architecture summary, sample audit trail, and a short recorded workflow demo.

## 90-day acquisition plan

### Weeks 1–2: measurement and trust

- Define GA4 conversions for `book_demo_click`, `signup_click`, `generate_lead`, and completed onboarding.
- Connect Google Search Console and Microsoft Clarity; create a weekly funnel report by source, landing page and conversion.
- Publish a security page covering approval tiers, allowlisting, encryption, data handling and audit logs.
- Replace any mock performance numbers with live platform numbers or clearly labeled interface examples.
- Add founder and team credibility, real LinkedIn profiles, response-time expectation, and pilot terms.

### Weeks 3–6: demand capture

- Build landing pages for: AI IT support India, Windows endpoint automation, self-healing IT, managed IT services Noida/Greater Noida, IT support for 50–500 employees, and secure employee offboarding.
- Publish two high-intent articles per month and one practical template or calculator per quarter.
- Create comparison pages for Intune and ManageEngine in addition to existing NinjaOne and Atera pages. Keep every claim sourced and dated.
- Set up Google Business Profile and submit consistent company details to relevant Indian business and B2B software directories.

### Weeks 7–12: outbound and partnerships

- Build a narrowly qualified list of IT managers, operations leaders and founders at 25–500 employee companies in NCR and other target metros.
- Run a five-touch email/LinkedIn sequence offering a 14-day endpoint health pilot, not a generic sales call.
- Partner with Microsoft 365 consultants, laptop resellers, cybersecurity firms and local MSPs that lack autonomous remediation.
- Launch small exact-match Google Search campaigns only after conversion tracking and dedicated landing pages are verified.
- Retarget consented visitors with demo and security-proof creative; do not run broad awareness ads initially.

## Offer ladder

1. Free secure-offboarding checklist or endpoint health scorecard.
2. 30-minute fleet assessment.
3. 14-day pilot on a limited device group with agreed success criteria.
4. Paid ASTRA rollout or managed IT engagement.

## Content themes

- Reduce repetitive Windows helpdesk tickets.
- Evidence-first AI versus unsupervised automation.
- Endpoint health, patching and offboarding checklists.
- Cost of downtime and technician time calculators.
- Real remediation walkthroughs with before/after telemetry.
- Security and compliance implications of AI-driven IT actions.

## Weekly scorecard

Track unique visitors, non-branded search clicks, lead-magnet conversion, demo booking rate, signup rate, qualified opportunities, pilot starts, pilot-to-paid conversion, customer acquisition cost, and pipeline by channel. Segment branded traffic from non-branded acquisition so growth is not overstated.

Initial targets should be treated as experiments, not promises: 2–4% visitor-to-lead on high-intent landing pages, 20–35% lead-to-qualified meeting, and 20%+ pilot-to-paid. Rebaseline after the first 100 qualified visits or 20 leads.
