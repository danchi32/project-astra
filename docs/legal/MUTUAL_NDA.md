## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to exchange information to evaluate and, if they choose, carry out
**[a pilot of the ASTRA platform / a commercial or technical partnership / describe]**
(the "**Purpose**"). This Agreement governs that exchange. It does not oblige either Party
to disclose anything or to enter into any further agreement.

## 2. Confidential Information

"**Confidential Information**" means all information disclosed by or on behalf of the
Discloser to the Recipient in connection with the Purpose, in any form, that is marked
confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. It includes
business plans, pricing, customer and prospect information, product roadmaps, software,
source code, architecture and security documentation, device and telemetry data, and the
existence and terms of the Parties' discussions.

It does not include information that the Recipient can show:

- (a) is or becomes public other than through the Recipient's breach;
- (b) was lawfully known to the Recipient without restriction before disclosure;
- (c) is received lawfully from a third party without a duty of confidence; or
- (d) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of the Confidential
  Information.

## 3. Obligations

The Recipient will:

- (a) use Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
- (b) not disclose it to anyone except its and its affiliates' directors, employees,
  contractors and professional advisers who need to know it for the Purpose and are bound
  by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as these ("**Representatives**").
  The Recipient is responsible for its Representatives' compliance;
- (c) protect it with at least the care it uses for its own confidential information of a
  similar kind, and no less than reasonable care;
- (d) not copy it except as needed for the Purpose, and not reverse engineer, decompile or
  analyse the composition of any software, prototype or sample provided; and
- (e) notify the Discloser promptly on becoming aware of any unauthorised use or
  disclosure, and cooperate to limit its effect.

## 4. Compelled disclosure

The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information where required by law, regulation, a
court or a competent authority, provided that, where lawful, it gives the Discloser prompt
notice, cooperates with any effort to obtain protective treatment, and discloses only what
is legally required.

## 5. Personal data

Neither Party intends to share personal data under this Agreement. If personal data is
shared (for example, device or user data during a pilot), the Recipient will process it
only for the Purpose, keep it secure, and comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection
Act, 2023 and other applicable law. Any pilot in which Technomate processes production
device data is governed by the ASTRA [Data Processing
Agreement](https://technomateai.com/dpa/).

## 6. Return and destruction

On the Discloser's written request, or when the Purpose ends, the Recipient will promptly
return or destroy the Confidential Information and confirm this in writing. The Recipient
may keep copies required by law or held in routine backups, which remain subject to this
Agreement until destroyed in the ordinary course.

## 7. No licence; no warranty

All Confidential Information remains the Discloser's property. No licence to any
intellectual property is granted except the limited right to use it for the Purpose.
Confidential Information is provided "as is", without warranty of accuracy or
completeness.

## 8. Term

This Agreement starts on the date above and covers disclosures made within **two (2)
years** of it, unless either Party ends it earlier on 30 days' written notice. The
obligations in sections 3 to 7 continue for **three (3) years** after the last disclosure,
and for source code, security documentation and trade secrets for as long as the
information remains confidential.

## 9. Remedies

A breach may cause harm for which damages are not an adequate remedy. The Discloser may
seek injunctive or other equitable relief in addition to any other remedy, without having
to prove actual damage.

## 10. Freedom to compete; no exclusivity

Nothing in this Agreement restricts either Party from developing, acquiring or marketing
products or services that compete with the other's, provided it does not use the other's
Confidential Information to do so. This Agreement creates no partnership, agency or
exclusivity.

## 11. Governing law and disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Any dispute arising out of or in
connection with it will be referred to a sole arbitrator appointed by mutual agreement
under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat and venue of arbitration is
Gautam Budh Nagar, Uttar Pradesh, and the language is English. Subject to that, the courts
at Gautam Budh Nagar, Uttar Pradesh have exclusive jurisdiction, and either Party may seek
urgent interim relief from any competent court.

## 12. General

This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject and supersedes
any earlier understanding. It may be amended only in writing signed by both Parties.
Neither Party may assign it without the other's written consent, except to a successor to
its business. If any provision is held unenforceable, the rest remains in effect. A
failure to enforce a right is not a waiver of it. It may be signed in counterparts and by
electronic signature, each of which is an original.
